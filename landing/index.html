FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How long does a typical project take?
Landing pages usually launch in 1–2 weeks, full websites in 3–4 weeks. ERP, data management, and custom platforms depend on scope — we give you a firm timeline after the discovery call.
Do you work with businesses that don't have a technical team?
Yes — most of our clients don't. We handle everything from planning to deployment and hand off a system that's simple to use, with training and documentation included.
Can you integrate with the tools we already use?
Yes. We regularly connect new systems to existing CRMs, accounting software, payment processors, and internal tools via APIs so nothing you rely on gets disrupted.
What does support look like after launch?
Every project includes a post-launch support window, and ongoing monitoring/maintenance plans are available for websites, ERP systems, and data platforms alike.
How is pricing determined for custom work?
After the discovery call, we send a fixed-price proposal based on scope — so you know the full cost upfront with no surprise invoices mid-project.
