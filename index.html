The Plan

6-Month Roadmap

Two parallel tracks — the website's technical/content SEO, and Instagram's organic growth — reviewed and adjusted monthly against what the data above actually shows.

Month 1

Foundation

  • Claim & verify Google Business Profile; complete every field (see GBP Content Pack)
  • Connect Google Search Console to the live domain so rankings become measurable
  • Replace placeholder address/phone with real details across site schema, GBP, and Instagram bio for NAP consistency
  • Start the WhatsApp review-request flow for every completed appointment
Month 2

Content Depth

  • Publish first 2 blog posts from the content calendar (keratin cost, bridal trial prep)
  • Add a dedicated bridal portfolio/gallery once real client photos are available
  • Expand the FAQ with 3 real questions pulled from actual customer WhatsApp/DM queries
Month 3

Local Citations

  • Submit the listing to 15–20 Indian local directories (JustDial, Sulekha, beauty/bridal directories)
  • Pursue 2–3 local backlinks — Chennai lifestyle blogs, wedding vendor directories for the Bridal Edit package
  • Re-check the keyword map against early Search Console query data and adjust priorities
Month 4

Conversion & Real Content

  • Swap remaining stock photography for real studio/client photos (with permission)
  • Review the Core Web Vitals trend from the dashboard above and address any regressions
  • Add a short client video testimonial or Reel embed if available
Month 5

Content Scaling

  • Publish 2 more blog posts (balayage vs. highlights, hair colour for Indian skin tones)
  • Add real AggregateRating schema once genuine Google reviews have accumulated — never before
  • Refresh service pricing on-page if anything has changed
Month 6

Review & Re-plan

  • Full 6-month trend review across this dashboard's Lighthouse and business-metrics history
  • Mine Search Console query data for keyword opportunities the original map missed
  • Set the next 6-month plan based on what the real data shows, not on assumptions
Month 1

Audit & Bio

  • Rewrite bio with service keywords + Chennai location + booking link to the live site
  • Geo-tag every post going forward; organize Highlights (Hair, Bridal, Skin, Reviews, Pricing)

Consistency

Reels Push

Month 4

UGC & Collaborations

  • Encourage client tagging/reposts (with permission) for social proof
  • Partner with 1–2 micro-influencers or bridal vendors (photographers, decorators) for cross-promotion
Month 5

Paid Test

  • Small-budget Meta ad test, geo-targeted to nearby Chennai neighbourhoods, promoting the Bridal Edit package
  • Measure against organic baseline before committing more budget
Month 6

Review & Scale

  • Analyse 6 months of Insights — reach, follower growth, best-performing content types
  • Double down on what worked; set content pillars for the next 6 months
Transparency

How This Dashboard Updates

Automated

Technical SEO scores

A GitHub Action runs every Monday, audits the live landing page with Lighthouse, and commits the result to this repo — no one has to remember to do it, and the numbers can't be quietly cherry-picked.

Manual, weekly

Business metrics

Google Business Profile, Instagram, and Search Console don't expose free public APIs for this — each requires the studio's own account access. These numbers are entered by hand each week from each platform's native Insights screen.
